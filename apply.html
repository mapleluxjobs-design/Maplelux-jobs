Terms & Conditions

1. Registration Fee: A one-time registration fee is required to process and verify your application. This fee specifically covers administrative costs, background checks, and the facilitation of linking your profile with potential employers.

2. Free Relocation: Once a successful employment offer is secured, MapleLux Jobs will provide free relocation facilitation to your place of work.

3. Refundable Clause (Guarantee): We stand by our service. If you are not successfully placed in a job within 90 days of your payment, you are eligible for a 100% full refund of your registration fee. To claim a refund, you must have attended all scheduled interviews and provided accurate information.

4. No Placement Guarantee: While we offer a refund, payment of the fee does not guarantee an immediate job offer.

5. Refund Process: Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.
